Meet Larrybash

01 — The Man & The Leader

About Larrybash

Rooted in Awori Traditional Authority

As the Seriki of Ota, Awori Land and traditional Chief of Staff & Spokesperson of the Olota-in-Council under HRM Oba (Prof.) Abdulkabir Adeyemi Obalanlege, Larrybash carries the trust of the traditional institution — a bridge between our heritage and proactive, modern representation.

Endorsed by the Forum of Awori Intelligentsia (FAI), which has called on all sons, daughters and residents of the constituency to send Larrybash to the National Assembly in 2027.

“I have a track record of full-fledged community and human capital development. I have engaged in this for decades before any aspiration for political office.”
— High Chief Taofeeq Olanrewaju Bashorun

Verified Background

  • Ansar-ud-Deen Grammar School, Ota — completed secondary education (1988/89 set), grounded from youth in the Ota community.

  • National Security Service — served with the National Security Guard and the NDLEA until 1999, building hands-on security and anti-narcotics expertise.

  • Private Security Entrepreneur — founded and ran private security operations in Lagos, protecting lives, businesses and livelihoods.

  • Executive & Union Leadership — leadership roles at Gokada and Chairman, Lagos Goat and Ram Butchers, managing two abattoirs and thousands of grassroots livelihoods.

  • Advanced Studies, United Kingdom — global exposure and executive education to match grassroots credibility with boardroom readiness.

  • Proven Peacemaker — in January 2025, alongside the Olota of Ota, he swiftly mediated a volatile clash in Ota, preventing a breakdown of law and order; and long before politics, he funded monthly stipends for elderly citizens, scholarships and hospital bills for indigent constituents.

02 — Where He Will Represent

Ado-Odo/Ota

Federal Constituency

Ado-Odo/Ota is the industrial heartbeat of Ogun State and one of Nigeria's most strategic constituencies. Anchored on the ancient Awori town of Ota, seat of the Olota of Ota, it stretches from the bustling Sango-Ota axis through Ijoko, Atan and Ado-Odo to the vast industrial estates of Agbara and Igbesa, where multinational manufacturers in food, beverages and pharmaceuticals operate on Awori soil.

Sharing a long boundary with Lagos State and serving as a gateway to the Republic of Benin through the Ota–Idiroko corridor, the constituency is home to millions of residents, traders and industrial workers across its 16 political wards. Yet a painful paradox persists, and resolving it is the heart of the Adele Adoko mandate.

“We host the factories, but we do not hold the jobs.”

03 — The Manifesto

The “Adele Adoko” Vision

“Áá délé, áá dókò” — Adele Adoko, a cherished Awori promise: we will reach every home and every farm; every community reached, every interest represented, no one left behind.

Three core pillars. One principle: action, not allocation — representation that shows up, delivers, and answers to the grassroots.

Pillar 1: Economic Empowerment & Industrial Synergy

Convene the Ado-Odo/Ota & Igbesa Industrial Summit within the first 100 days to formalise a “Host Community First” employment framework with the factories on our soil; revive technical education — the Igbesa Technical School and the ILO facility — so our youths graduate with the exact skills industry demands; and champion micro-grants and modern market infrastructure for market women and artisans.

Pillar 2: Proactive Social Welfare

Institutionalise constituency food banks so no family is left behind, expand scholarship programmes for indigent students across all 16 wards, and provide healthcare assistance that reaches the elderly, widows and the vulnerable — proactively, not reactively.

I will not wait for government allocation before giving my people what they deserve.

— High Chief T. O. Bashorun (Larrybash)

Pillar 3: Security & Community Resilience

Leveraging decades of NDLEA and private security experience — and building on his past donations of a Hilux patrol van and motorcycles to the Amotekun Corps — he will institutionalise logistical support for grassroots security, establish anti-cultism youth rehabilitation centres, and advocate specialised federal deployments for our border and industrial corridors.

Our Commitment

Clean. Issue-Based. Peaceful.

The Adele Adoko movement wins through superior ideas, deep empathy and unyielding discipline — never through intimidation. Every volunteer, online and on the streets, upholds this code:

Zero Violence

No thuggery, no intimidation. We de-escalate, walk away, and report — we never retaliate.

Issue-Based

We debate policies, records and capacity — never the personalities or families of our opponents.

Truth Online

No fake news, no cyberbullying, no tribal or religious slurs. We verify before we share.

Respect for All

We honour our elders, traditional institutions and every faith and ethnicity across the constituency.

Aligned with INEC guidelines and the ethics of the All Progressives Congress.

Privacy Notice

The Larrybash Campaign Organisation (Larrybash Secretariat, Okede, Ota, Ogun State) is the data controller for the information you provide on this site, and is committed to protecting it under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), 2023.

What we collect

Your name, WhatsApp/phone number, ward, area of interest, and (optionally) your email address. Your number is confirmed by a one-time code.

Why, and our lawful basis

Solely to send you campaign updates, mobilisation materials and your ward group link. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box — never a pre-ticked default.

How we secure it

Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and held in an access-controlled database available only to authorised campaign staff. We never collect card or financial details, and we do not sell your data.

Who sees it

Only the campaign's mobilisation and contact-centre team, for the purposes above. It is not shared with third parties for unrelated use, and it is never sold.

Who processes it for us

We use trusted service providers who process your data only on our instructions, under data-processing agreements:

  • ContactZero — our campaign contact-centre platform.
  • Twilio / Meta (WhatsApp) — delivery of SMS and WhatsApp messages.
  • Supabase — secure, access-controlled database hosting.

How long we keep it

Until the end of the 2027 election cycle or until you opt out — whichever is sooner — after which it is securely deleted.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent (opt out) at any time — as easily as you gave it. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).
